Movies

Here are some recs for movies, shown in no particular order:

Everything Everywhere All At Once

This is a sci-fi action comedy-drama film about a woman who discovers that there are infinite universes and that she can travel between them. At times it's deep, at times it's funny, and at times it's just plain weird. But that's what makes it amazing.

I highly recommend this movie!

The Mitchells vs. the Machines

An animated sci-fi coming of age film about a family who must save the world from a robot uprising... except they are the most dysfunctional family ever.

This is my favorite movie of all time, it is a must watch for anyone and everyone. I highly recommend this movie and you must watch it. *taps you with my wand forcing you to watch it*

Ferris Bueller's Day Off

A high school student decides to skip school and have a day of fun with his girlfriend and best friend.

This is another one of my all time favotites! It is a classic film that is always hilarious and fun to watch.
