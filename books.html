Books

Here are some of my favorite books. If you are looking for something to read, check them out!

The Hunger Games

This is a dystopian novel about a society where children are forced to fight to the death in a televised event.

This is a great book if you like dystopian novels or are interested in the concept of survival of the fittest.

Arc of a Scythe

This is a fantasy novel about a young person who becomes a scythe, a reaper who kills people to maintain balance in the world.

This is a great book if you like fantasy novels or are interested in the concept of death and mortality.
