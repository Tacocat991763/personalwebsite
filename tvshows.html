TV Shows

Here are some of my favorite TV shows. If you are looking for something to watch, check them out! They aren't listed in any particular order, but Dr. Who is my favorite.

Dr. Who

This is one of my favorite TV shows. I have only seen doctors 9 and 10. But I will be watching older episodes in the future.

This is a classic sci-fi series, if you like sci-fi or soap opera like shows this is a must-watch!

The Crown

This is a historical drama about the reign of Queen Elizabeth II.

This is a great show if you like historical dramas or are interested in the British monarchy.

Stranger Things

This is a sci-fi horror series set in the 1980s. It follows a group of kids as they try to find their friend who has gone missing.

This is a great show if you like sci-fi, horror, or 80s nostalgia.

I have seen all seasons multiple times and highly recommend this show. I ship Mike and Will and you should too.
